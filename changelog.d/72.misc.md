`scripts/ci-local.sh` runs the cheap `.github/workflows/ci.yml` gates on a developer machine from one declarative gate registry, distinguishing a skipped gate (exit 2 — no signing token, no towncrier config) from a genuine pass, and its `drift` gate (`scripts/ci-drift-check.sh`) fails in either direction if that registry and `ci.yml`'s step list stop matching. Packaging, the plugin validator and the Playwright e2e suite are out of scope and allowlisted with reasons.
