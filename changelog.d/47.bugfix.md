Alertmanager evidence now reaches dot-ai instead of `Current` reporting `Alertmanager datasource missing` on every install. The lookup passes `all: true`, because Grafana's built-in Alertmanager datasource declares no metrics/logs/tracing/annotations/alerting capability and was filtered out of every result, and alerts are read from the datasource's own `/api/v2/alerts` route — `/alertmanager/api/v2/alerts` for a Mimir or Cortex implementation — because `AlertManagerDatasource.query()` is a stub that always returns no data. A failed or forbidden read is reported as unavailable rather than as no alerts.
