Show-me navigation Asks (e.g. "show me the logs") now resolve in zero dot-ai hops when there is Grafana evidence to point at, and fail loudly instead of claiming evidence they do not hold: an explicit error is returned when "Send Grafana evidence" is off, when the Grafana stack read failed, and when the read succeeded but returned nothing with no drilldown links. Diagnosis language (why, error, crash, analyze, and related tokens) always forces the normal dot-ai hop instead of the 0-hop skip.
