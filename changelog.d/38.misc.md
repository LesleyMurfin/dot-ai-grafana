Dependabot no longer proposes major bumps for `eslint` in the eslint group. `eslint-plugin-react`, a direct devDependency also required by `@grafana/eslint-config`, caps its eslint peer range at `^9.7`, so a major fails `npm ci` with ERESOLVE, as it did in #38. Lift once `eslint-plugin-react` ships an ESLint 10 peer range (tracked upstream in jsx-eslint/eslint-plugin-react#3977, fix PR #4022, both open).
